07 · Key takeaways & V5 decisions

What the arithmetic forces

Seven findings from the notebook + PDF §13.

DP replicates everything

480 GB/GPU forever. Six cards just to hold state, before activations.

ZeRO-1/2 still miss

142.5 / 84.4 GB at 32 GPUs in-sim. Theory: ZeRO-2 fits only from 32 (73.1 GB).

ZeRO-3 wins

26.3 GB/GPU simulated at 32; fits from 8 GPUs with room for activations.

Comm trade: 2P to 3P

Extra forward/backward all-gathers: 120 to 180 GB per GPU per step.

Overlap from step one

Bucketed reduce-scatter during backward hides the transfer; log the ratio early.

FP8 is a speed play

-12% state, but the prize is matmul, activations and comm volume.

Starting point: ZeRO-2 on 32 or ZeRO-3 on 8?

Needs a measured step time for both on the real architecture, with activation memory included. The 9x NVLink vs InfiniBand gap decides how much traffic must stay inside a node.

8-bit from the start?

Short bf16 vs MXFP8 runs on the same architecture, comparing loss and step time. TorchTitan reports up to 41% faster on B200 with matching 1,500-step loss.

Any state to CPU/NVMe?

Only if memory-bound after the stage is chosen — offload converts a memory problem into a PCIe-bandwidth problem (~60 GB/s).
